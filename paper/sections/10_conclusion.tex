\section{Conclusion}

The project currently has a frozen, executable cross-simulator contract for a
compact tractor--semitrailer and loading dock. Shared state, command, geometry,
configuration, provenance, safety-supervision, and conformance-validation code
is implemented and tested. The next milestone is the two-dimensional kinematic
state-transition model and its quantitative validation. Conclusions about PPO,
NMPC, robustness, or MuJoCo transfer are deferred until reproducible experiments
exist.
