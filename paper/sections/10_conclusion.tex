\section{Conclusion}

The project currently has a frozen, executable cross-simulator contract for a
compact tractor--semitrailer and loading dock. Shared state, command, geometry,
configuration, provenance, safety-supervision, and conformance-validation code
is implemented and tested. A configuration-generated MuJoCo vehicle, dock, and
shared-state adapter now satisfy the model-side contract and low-speed
engineering checks. The two-dimensional kinematic state-transition model is now
implemented and quantitatively validated, including direct low-speed
cross-backend trajectory checks. Shared path and task geometry and the
Gymnasium environment are now implemented and tested, including a separately
versioned full-size service-yard interaction scenario. A PPO training
framework over that task, on either plant, is implemented and software-tested
but has produced no tuned or evaluated policy. The next milestones are the
NMPC baseline and PPO tuning on the two-dimensional task.
Conclusions about controller performance, robustness, or transfer are deferred
until reproducible experiments exist.
