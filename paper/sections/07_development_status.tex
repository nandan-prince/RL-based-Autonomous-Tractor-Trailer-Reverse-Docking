The PPO training framework exercises the observation/action loop end to end
on both plants, but with untuned settings and without a registered experiment.
The immediate gates are the NMPC baseline, which enters through the runner's
controller hook, and tuning PPO on the two-dimensional task. The task
definition already runs on MuJoCo; comparing its event boundaries with the
two-dimensional plant under shared scenarios remains the event-level transfer
gate.
