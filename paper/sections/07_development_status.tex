The PPO training framework exercises the observation/action loop end to end on
both plants, while LQR and NMPC exercise the shared classical-controller
contract in the compact 2D task. PPO optimization and task-reward settings are
now explicit, validated, and recorded. Classical-controller settings, split
ownership, formal evidence output, and curriculum gates are also versioned,
and curved-path/reward-study inputs are controlled, but remain untuned. No comparative
experiment is registered. The immediate gate is paired curved and randomized
2D evaluation of PPO and NMPC. Comparing task-event boundaries between the 2D
and MuJoCo plants under shared scenarios remains the transfer gate.
